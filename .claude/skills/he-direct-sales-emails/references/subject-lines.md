# Subject-Line + Preview Swipe File

Every example line in this file is a PATTERN. Do not reuse the wording. Derive the equivalent in the
user's voice from `references/voice-and-examples.md`.

The rule that beat everything else: **subject = hook, preview = offer.** The subject buys the open with
curiosity, a personal question, news, or a bold outcome. The preview text carries the price, the discount,
and the deadline. Write 5 subject candidates for every email, then flag the recommended A and B pair.

Each template below is `SUBJECT` -> `PREVIEW`. Replace `{name}`, `{outcome}`, `{painful task}`,
`{product}`, `{price}`, `{deadline}`, `{discount}` with the real values for this offer.

## Openers (personal / question / story)
- `Hey {name}` -> "Can I {do their painful task} for you before {deadline}?"
  (illustrative pattern: "Hey {name}" -> "Can I automate your social media for you BEFORE Wednesday?",
  historically one of the highest-opening formats available)
- `can I help you {get the outcome}?` -> "follow these 3 steps first"
- `this scares me {name}` -> "should I press it or not?"
- `{provocative outcome}` -> "{what they get}. {price} before {deadline}."
  (illustrative pattern: "Cancel your marketing agency tomorrow" -> "{N}+ done-for-you assets. {price}
  before Thursday.")
- `{small personal detail}` -> "{tie it to the offer}"

## Why-now / news / persona
- `Your {product} {version} is here` -> "{get the outcome} now (at {discount})"
  (illustrative pattern: "Your {product} 2.0 is here" -> "hire your new {category} now (at a deep
  discount)", historically the top unit-count performer in this format)
- `like {familiar tool}, but {key differentiator}` -> "{product}: new {feature} update!"
- `hey {name}, it's {product/AI name}` -> "{proof the product just did something for them}"
  (illustrative pattern: "hey {name}. it's {AI name}." -> "I {did a concrete thing} while you were reading
  this subject line", historically the top revenue performer in this format)
- `my team is obsessed with {these}` -> "and the kind of buyer it brings you"

## Mechanism / proof
- `here's exactly what {each week/Friday} looks like` -> "the {N} {weeks}, mapped out"
- `what you'll have built by {date}` -> "{concrete deliverable}"
- `my team works with you for just ${price}` -> "follow these 3 steps first"

## Disqualifier
- `this isn't for you if...` -> "read this before you decide"
- `you probably don't need everything` -> (site-wide sale; no preview needed; a consistently strong
  performer for this format)
- `sorry, this probably isn't for you` -> "a 30-second gut-check before {deadline}"

## Urgency / closers (tight clock = better)
- `[{N} hours!] then GONE` -> "last chance tbh"
- `Final countdown: {offer} ends tonight` -> "GONE at midnight"
- `final warning {name}` -> "act in the next 60 minutes"
- `final chance {name}` -> "{N} mins till {code/price} expires"
- `+${X} at midnight` -> "last day at ${price}."
- `this closes tonight` -> "last chance to join"
- `You have only 1 day left (enrollment closes tomorrow)` -> "{product}"
- `we're live in {N} hours` -> "{event} starts at {time}. last call"

## Scarcity / cohort
- `opening {N} spots` -> "{aspirational lifestyle line}"
  (illustrative pattern: "opening 20 spots" -> "I can work from anywhere. Or not work at all on a
  Tuesday.")

## Post-event / warm
- `Here's the {secret/replay} link we mentioned...` -> "Expires soon" (historically a strong click-rate
  performer for this format)
- `psssss... this just happened` -> "You haven't missed it. Here's how to still get in."

## A/B pairing strategy (a pattern worth repeating)
Run a **story / curiosity Subject A** against a **number / outcome Subject B** for the same email.
Illustrative pairs:
- "my team is obsessed with these"  vs  "the {low price} product that runs without me"
- "I burned a lot of money and time on this"  vs  "from burned budget to a repeatable system. this is HOW"
- "this closes tomorrow at midnight"  vs  "the upgrade most people miss"

## Open-rate notes (so you set expectations and pick wisely)
- Casual, lowercase, first-name, minimal-emoji subjects tend to open BEST, with plain personal questions
  usually the strongest performers of all.
- Emoji + hard-urgency subjects tend to open a little lower but convert well as closers.
- Typical ranges for a healthy list: cold full-list 25-35% opens, engaged segment 45-55%, opener/clicker
  re-targets 65-80%. Re-targets open high but rarely close on their own; see sequence rules.
