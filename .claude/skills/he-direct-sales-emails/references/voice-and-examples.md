# Voice and Examples

## Tone and style rules

- First person, singular where a real person is signing. Write like one person talking to one person.
- Plain language, around a grade 8 reading level. Short words beat long ones every time.
- Short paragraphs. One idea per paragraph. It has to read on a phone.
- Warm and direct, not formal and not breathless. Respect on both sides of the sentence.
- Concrete over abstract. A named program, a real number, a specific afternoon.
- Vary sentence length on purpose. A short line next to a longer one.
- No em dashes anywhere.
- Ask once, clearly, and make the ask easy to say yes to.

## Banned words and phrases

- delve, leverage, robust, seamless, utilize, harness, showcase, elevate, unlock, empower, foster,
  streamline, holistic, synergy, vibrant, tapestry, journey as a metaphor, dive in, dive deep
- "In today's fast-paced world", "Moreover", "Furthermore", "Additionally", "It's worth noting that",
  "At the end of the day"
- "the less fortunate", "those in need", "give back", "make a difference", "change lives"
- "Your gift of any size" as a substitute for a real suggested amount
- "URGENT", all-caps shouting, and any deadline that is not real
- "click here" as link text

## Sign-off style

- Signed by a named person with their role, not by the organization as a faceless sender.
- Default close: a short line of thanks, then the name and role.
- P.S. is used deliberately, for the deadline, the monthly-giving option, or the payment detail.
  Not for a second unrelated ask.
