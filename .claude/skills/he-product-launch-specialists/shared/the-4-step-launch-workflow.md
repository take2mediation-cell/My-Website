# The 4-Step Launch Workflow — Shared Framework for All Skills

> **Read this whenever the user needs the big picture.** This is the canonical
> explanation of how the AI Product Launch Specialists plugin takes a Claude
> Plugin from idea to live, money-making, low-ticket AI product. Every skill must
> be able to explain this workflow on request — and proactively, when it would
> help the user orient.

---

## When to explain this workflow

Explain it (briefly, then in full if they want) whenever:

- the user **asks** how it works, what the steps are, where to start, what's
  next, or how to launch/sell a Claude Plugin;
- the user is **new** or seems unsure where they are in the journey;
- a skill **finishes its step** and it helps to point at the next one;
- the plugin **deems it important** for the user to understand the path ahead.

Don't force it into every reply. Lead with the one-line overview; expand to the
full step breakdown only when the user wants depth or context calls for it.

---

## What this workflow produces

A **Claude Plugin sold as a low-ticket AI product** — researched, built, sold,
and promoted, end to end. (Reminder: in this plugin, **AI Product = Claude
Plugin** — see [`what-is-a-claude-plugin.md`](what-is-a-claude-plugin.md).)

A **low-ticket AI product** is a self-serve, impulse-to-mid priced Claude Plugin
the buyer can purchase without a sales call — sold on autopilot through a funnel
and fed by ads.

## The workflow at a glance

```
Step 1            Step 2            Step 3                 Step 4
RESEARCH    →     CREATE      →     SELL            →      PROMOTE
Product          Product           Sales Funnel           Ads
Research         Creation          Creation               Launch
│                │                 │                      │
Offer DNA  →     Installable  →    Live funnel      →     Live ad
+ docs in        ZIP plugin        (page, checkout,       campaigns
```

|------|------------------|--------------|

## Step 1 — Product Research

**Headline:** Know What Claude Plugin To Create And Sell (Before You Even Create
It)

**Outcome:** All elements and attributes of your low-ticket offer and Claude
Plugin are defined and nicely documented in Notion.

**Specialist:** `he-product-researcher` *(active in V1)*
**You provide → you get:** your niche/audience info → real market research, the
top 5 different Claude Plugin ideas, and a complete Offer DNA for the one you
pick, saved to Notion (or Google Docs, or delivered in chat).

---

## Step 2 — Product Creation

**Headline:** Create Your Claude Plugin In One Day (Without Being A Developer)

**Outcome:** A fully built, tested, sellable Claude Plugin in the ZIP —
installable inside Claude, ready to deliver personalized value to a buyer, plus a
user guide in text version.

**Specialist:** `he-product-creator` *(active)*
**You provide → you get:** your Offer DNA → a finished, tested Claude Plugin
packaged as an installable ZIP, with a written user guide.

---

## Step 3 — Sales Funnel Creation

**Headline:** Turn Your Claude Plugin Into A 24/7 Money Machine

**Outcome:** A live, end-to-end sales funnel for your Claude Plugin — sales page,
checkout page, upsell, and follow-up — selling on autopilot.

**Specialist:** `he-sales-funnel-specialist` *(active)*
**You provide → you get:** your plugin + Offer DNA → all funnel copy (sales page,
checkout, upsell, follow-up emails) wired into a live, self-serve funnel.

---

## Step 4 — Ads Launch

**Headline:** Reach And Get Buyers On Autopilot

**Outcome:** All the Meta ad copy needed to launch — a campaign structure, Text
Hooks (the overlay text for your image/video ads), and Primary Text — written and
ready to drop into Ads Manager to start sending buyers to your funnel.

**Specialist:** `he-meta-ads-specialist` *(active)*
**You provide → you get:** your offer + funnel → a Meta campaign structure, Text
Hooks for each ad, and shared Primary Text — high-converting ad copy ready to
launch. *(This step writes the copy; it does not design the images/videos or run
the campaigns for you.)*
